Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Nimboo Technologies Private Limited collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Nimboo Customer mobile application.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nimboo Technologies Private Limited ("Nimboo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Nimboo Customer mobile application ("App"). By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Phone number (used as your login identity).
  • Full name and email address, when provided by you or through supported sign-in methods.
  • Information received through Google Sign-In, such as your name and email address, as permitted by your Google account settings.
  • Delivery addresses you save in the App.

1.2 Order & Transaction Information

  • Products added to cart and purchased.
  • Order history, status, and delivery details.
  • Payment method type (we do not store full card numbers).

1.3 Device & Technical Information

  • Your IP address and basic connection details, which our servers receive automatically whenever the App contacts them.
  • Server-side logs of requests, kept for security, fraud prevention, and debugging.

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not collect advertising identifiers.

1.4 Location Information

  • If you tap "Use Current Location" and grant permission, we read your device's GPS location to fill in a delivery address and to show stores that deliver to you.
  • Location is only read while you are actively using the App. We never collect location in the background.
  • Location access is optional. You can type your address manually instead, and you may revoke access in your device settings at any time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Verify your identity and manage your account via OTP authentication and supported sign-in methods.
  • Process your orders and facilitate delivery to your address.
  • Send order confirmation, delivery status, and important account notifications.
  • Improve our App, services, and customer experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.
  • Respond to your customer support requests.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With delivery riders: Your name, phone number, and delivery address are shared with the rider assigned to your order.
  • With store partners: Order details are shared with the store fulfilling your order.
  • With our SMS provider (MSG91): Your phone number is passed to MSG91 solely so it can deliver your one-time login password.
  • With payment processors: We use trusted third-party payment gateways that are PCI-DSS compliant.
  • With service providers: Hosting, mapping/geocoding, and infrastructure partners may process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or government regulation.

These providers act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data transmitted between the App and our servers.
  • Secure OTP-based authentication (no passwords stored).
  • Access controls limiting who within our organisation can access your data.
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability testing.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your device secure and not share your OTP with anyone.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services.

5.1 Deleting your account in the App

You can delete your account and personal data yourself, at any time, from Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and removes:

  • Your profile — name, email address, and phone number.
  • All saved delivery addresses and location data.
  • Your cart, order history, and previously-bought list.
  • Ratings and reviews you submitted.
  • Notification history and any registered device tokens.

5.2 What we must keep

Invoices and tax records for completed orders are retained for the period Indian tax law requires, and anonymised records may be kept for fraud prevention and accounting. These are unlinked from your profile and cannot be used to identify you.

5.3 Requesting deletion another way

You can also request deletion by emailing nimboo.in@gmail.com from your registered number. We action such requests within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information (via Profile → Edit).
  • Delete your account and personal data yourself (via Profile → Delete Account).
  • Withdraw consent for location access via your device settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of the above rights, contact us at nimboo.in@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Nimboo is a general-audience shopping app intended for adults aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

Our App may contain links to or integrate with third-party services (e.g., payment gateways and map services). These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or via the registered contact details. Your continued use of the App after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.
