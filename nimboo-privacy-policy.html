Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Nimboo Technologies Private Limited collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Nimboo Customer mobile application.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nimboo Technologies Private Limited ("Nimboo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Nimboo Customer mobile application ("App"). By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Google Sign-In and Account Information

Nimboo uses Google Sign-In to allow you to register and sign in to your Nimboo account. When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information made available through the Google Sign-In service, such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google account identifier.
  • Your profile picture, if made available through the sign-in service and used by Nimboo.

We use this information to authenticate you, create and manage your Nimboo account, and associate your orders and preferences with your account. Nimboo does not receive or store your Google account password.

1.2 Delivery & Profile Information

  • Delivery addresses you save in the App.
  • Profile information you choose to provide or update.

1.3 Order & Transaction Information

  • Products added to cart and purchased.
  • Order history, status, and delivery details.
  • Payment method type. Nimboo does not store full card numbers.

1.4 Device & Technical Information

  • Your IP address and basic connection details, which our servers receive automatically whenever the App contacts them.
  • Server-side logs of requests, kept for security, fraud prevention, and debugging.

The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not collect advertising identifiers.

1.5 Location Information

  • If you tap "Use Current Location" and grant permission, we read your device's GPS location to fill in a delivery address and to show stores that deliver to you.
  • Location is only read while you are actively using the App. We never collect location in the background.
  • Location access is optional. You can type your address manually instead, and you may revoke access in your device settings at any time.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you and manage your Nimboo account using Google Sign-In.
  • Process your orders and facilitate delivery to your address.
  • Send order confirmation, delivery status, and important account notifications.
  • Improve our App, services, and customer experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.
  • Respond to your customer support requests.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With delivery riders: Your name, phone number if provided for delivery, and delivery address are shared with the rider assigned to your order.
  • With store partners: Relevant order details are shared with the store fulfilling your order.
  • With payment processors: We use trusted third-party payment gateways to process payments. Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies.
  • With service providers: Hosting, mapping/geocoding, and infrastructure partners may process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or government regulation.

These providers act on our instructions where applicable and may not use your data for unrelated purposes. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data transmitted between the App and our servers.
  • Secure authentication using Google Sign-In.
  • Access controls limiting who within our organisation can access your data.
  • Security assessments and vulnerability testing where appropriate.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your device secure and protect access to your Google account.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services.

5.1 Deleting your account in the App

You can delete your account and personal data yourself, at any time, from Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and removes personal information and account-associated data that we are not required to retain.

  • Your Nimboo profile information.
  • Saved delivery addresses and associated location data.
  • Your cart and account-associated preferences.
  • Ratings and reviews you submitted, where applicable.
  • Notification history and registered device tokens, where applicable.

5.2 What we may retain

Invoices and tax records for completed orders may be retained for the period Indian tax law requires. Certain records may also be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or legal compliance. Where appropriate, retained records may be anonymised or separated from your active account.

5.3 Requesting deletion another way

You can also request account deletion by emailing nimboo.in@gmail.com. Please send the request from the email address associated with your Nimboo account where possible. We will process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our retention obligations.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and personal data, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw permission for location access through your device settings.
  • Contact us regarding concerns about our data practices.

To exercise any of the above rights, contact us at nimboo.in@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Nimboo is a general-audience shopping app intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

Nimboo integrates with third-party services necessary to provide authentication, payments, mapping, hosting, and other app functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

In particular, Google Sign-In is used for authentication. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google processes information according to Google's applicable privacy practices and your Google account settings.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services you use.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or via the registered contact details where appropriate. Your continued use of the App after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.
